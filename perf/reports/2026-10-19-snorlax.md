# snorlax, 2026-10-19

`python3 perf/report.py results` with `BENCH_CAMERA=replay`. Seeed AGX Orin 64 GB, L4T r36.4.4, MAXN. The scene camera
is replay_camera.py playing 60 frames recorded on abra, so latencies leave out the camera driver. Rates here are what a
subscriber *received*: snorlax had the default 208 KB UDP receive buffer and lost 20-40% of large messages, while the
nodes logged ~10 Hz (detector) and 5 Hz (SAM3, BoxerNet). See Developer setup, Raise the DDS socket buffers.
