# abra, 2026-10-19

`python3 perf/report.py results` after the overnight runs. AGX Orin 64 GB devkit, L4T r36.4.7, MAXN, Gemini 336L
scene camera, sha-8091bbee perception images. Runs without `blas1` in their name predate the OpenBLAS fix. Uncapped
SAM3 sections: SAM3 at its default 15 Hz cap. "RAM over idle" is unreliable on abra (it held ~18 GB unaccounted for at
idle); use the snorlax report for memory. Write-up: rammp-org.github.io, Perception compute budget.
NanoOWL image rows (rammp-perception#12) were added in the afternoon, after its quality check (`quality/`); the
finalized YOLOE-26m stack was rerun then as their baseline, and both runs are shown.
